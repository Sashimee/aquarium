# Fish behaviour model

One line per behaviour: what the code does, where the idea or number comes from, and the
parameter that controls it. Parameters in `STEERING` live in `src/world.ts`; per-species values
come from the `behaviour` and `locomotion` blocks of `content/species/*.json`. Rows marked
**heuristic** are tuned by eye or by the tests rather than taken from a measurement. Treat them as
open to correction.

## Conventions

- **Tank frame:** the world's config picks it, and food, startles, decor, anchors and snapshots
  all use it. With `space` it is tank space from `@aquarium/shared` (metres, y = 0 under the
  bottom glass, the back pane at −Z, the viewer at +Z), the scene's own frame, and the floor is
  the substrate heightfield (`sampleFloor`). With the legacy `tank` it is a box centred on the
  substrate surface with a flat floor at y = 0.
- **Body frame:** +Z forward, +Y dorsal, +X the fish's left. Positive yaw turns left, positive
  pitch raises the nose, positive roll lowers the right flank (`quatFromYawPitchRoll`).
- **`depthBand`:** `[low, high]` as fractions of the water column measured from the substrate:
  0 is the sand under the fish and 1 is the surface, so on sloped sand the band follows the slope.
- **Step:** fixed 30 Hz (`STEP_HZ`). All randomness comes from the seeded sfc32 `Rng`, so the
  same seed and inputs reproduce the same trajectory bit for bit on one JS engine.
- **Snapshot:** `Float32Array`, `SNAPSHOT_STRIDE = 11` floats per fish: position xyz,
  quaternion xyzw, speed (m/s), yaw rate (rad/s, for tail-beat and bend), state (`FishState`),
  thrust (0–1 fraction of maximum caudal thrust; 0 while coasting).
- **Species parameters:** `BEHAVIOUR` in `src/behaviour.ts` holds every species-behaviour
  tunable. Behaviours the shared schema has no field for are switched on by
  `behaviour.traits` (`TRAITS` in `src/species.ts`): `shoal`, `air-gulp`, `glass-grazer`,
  `rock-grazer`, `anemone-host`, `cave-dweller`, `flare-display`. An unknown or conflicting
  trait fails species loading. `gait: hover` gives a fish a self-chosen station.
- **Per-trait parameters:** a species can override some of those defaults with optional
  `behaviour` objects, read by `toSimSpecies`. A field left out keeps the `BEHAVIOUR` default.
  `airGulp.perHour` → `gulpsPerHour` (needs `air-gulp` and `gait: forage`);
  `grazing.meanIntervalS`/`minS`/`maxS` → `grazeMeanIntervalS`/`grazeMinS`/`grazeMaxS` (needs
  `rock-grazer` on a free swimmer: no home, gait neither `hover` nor `forage`); `flare.minS`/`maxS`
  → `flareMinS`/`flareMaxS` (needs `flare-display`); `station.radiusBodyLengths`/`restationMinS`/
  `restationMaxS` → `stationRadiusBodyLengths`/`restationMinS`/`restationMaxS` (needs a home:
  `gait: hover`, `anemone-host` or `cave-dweller`, because an anemone or cave fish placed without
  its anchor holds a self-chosen station instead); `refuge.afterStartleS` → `shelterAfterStartleS`
  (needs `cave-dweller`); `excursion.meanIntervalS`/`leashBodyLengths` →
  `excursionMeanIntervalS`/`hostLeashBodyLengths` (needs `anemone-host`). `station`, `refuge` and
  `excursion` also need a gait other than `forage` and no `glass-grazer`, since those behaviours
  take precedence over a home. Species loading **rejects** an object the sim would never read,
  because it would otherwise be ignored silently and a warning in a worker is easy to miss. It also rejects a min/max pair that is out of order after
  the merge, such as `grazing: { minS: 50 }` against the default `grazeMaxS` of 4.
- **Feeding:** every feeding tunable is in `FEEDING` (`src/feeding.ts`). Food arrives each tick
  as `[id, x, y, z, typeIndex]` float32 records (`FOOD_STRIDE = 5`), `typeIndex` in the order of
  the shared `foodTypeSchema` (`FOOD_TYPES`). The sim never moves food; it reports
  `ate {fishId, foodId, portion}` and gameplay removes what was eaten. Portions are the lifecycle
  model's feed portions; `FOOD_PARTICLES` gives what one particle of each type holds. A species'
  `lifecycle.diet` lists foods most-preferred first.

### Circling bound

Tangs boxed in by rock and glass sometimes turn through a full 360° within 4 s: a turn out of a
dead end, or a lap round a rock. The tests measure each loop's radius as half the smallest span of
its path and allow none tighter than 0.35 body lengths and fewer than 2 loops per 10 fish-minutes.
Measured over seeds 1–8: the tightest yellow tang loop is 0.39 L at 0.33 loops per 10
fish-minutes, and the tightest blue tang loop is 0.40 L at 0.46 (before the turning-radius cap
they were 0.20 L and 0.17 L).

### Tang swimming mode

Both tangs carry `swimmingMode: labriform`: surgeonfishes (Acanthuridae) cruise on flapping
pectoral fins and keep the body and tail for bursts (Walker & Westneat 1997, J Exp Biol 200:1549).
PLAN §2.3 lists tangs under the carangiform spine wave, so the renderer should check which one it
uses.

## Feeding

Fish state while chasing, striking or rasping food is `FishState.Feed` (12). No new species
fields: where a fish feeds comes from its `depthBand` and `gait`, what it eats from
`lifecycle.diet`.

## Not yet modelled

Area-restricted search after a find, learning which food is good, and food type affecting the
strike (suction versus biting) are not modelled. Following a finger or laser pointer on the glass (curiosity) and
memory of feeding spots are not modelled yet (#72). Behaviour constants outside the per-trait
parameter objects are shared by every species.
