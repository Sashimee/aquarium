# Fish behaviour model

One line per behaviour: what the code does, where the idea or number comes from, and the
parameter that controls it. Parameters in `STEERING` live in `src/world.ts`; per-species values
come from the `behaviour` and `locomotion` blocks of `content/species/*.json`. Rows marked
**heuristic** are tuned by eye or by the tests rather than taken from a measurement. Treat them as
open to correction.

## Conventions

- **Tank frame:** metres, origin at the centre of the substrate surface, +Y up to the water
  surface, X across the front glass, Z front to back.
- **Body frame:** +Z forward, +Y dorsal, +X the fish's left. Positive yaw turns left, positive
  pitch raises the nose, positive roll lowers the right flank (`quatFromYawPitchRoll`).
- **`depthBand`:** `[low, high]` as fractions of the water column measured from the substrate:
  0 is the floor and 1 is the surface.
- **Step:** fixed 30 Hz (`STEP_HZ`). All randomness comes from the seeded sfc32 `Rng`, so the
  same seed and inputs reproduce the same trajectory bit for bit on one JS engine.
- **Snapshot:** `Float32Array`, `SNAPSHOT_STRIDE = 11` floats per fish: position xyz,
  quaternion xyzw, speed (m/s), yaw rate (rad/s, for tail-beat and bend), state (`FishState`),
  thrust (0–1 fraction of maximum caudal thrust; 0 while coasting).
- **Species parameters:** `BEHAVIOUR` in `src/behaviour.ts` holds every species-behaviour
  tunable. Behaviours the shared schema has no field for are switched on by
  `behaviour.traits` (`TRAITS` in `src/species.ts`): `shoal`, `air-gulp`, `glass-grazer`,
  `rock-grazer`, `anemone-host`, `cave-dweller`, `flare-display`. An unknown or conflicting
  trait fails species loading. `gait: hover` gives a fish a self-chosen station.

| Steering architecture                   | Each fish sums desired-velocity terms; an imminent wall scales the other terms down (prioritised blending).                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Reynolds 1987, "Flocks, herds and schools", SIGGRAPH 21(4):25; Reynolds 1999, "Steering behaviors for autonomous characters", GDC.   | `avoidPriority` 0.5 (**heuristic**)                                                                                                     |

| Mass, thrust and drag                   | Mass scales with L³; quadratic drag ½ρC·L²·v²; thrust capped at the drag at max speed, so terminal speed equals max speed. | Turn-rate and pitch limits              | Yaw and pitch follow a P-controller clamped to the species' turn rate; pitch is clamped to the species' limit.                                                                                                                                                                                              | Species data; cruising (not C-start) turn rates. Controller gain and smoothing are **heuristic**.                                    | `locomotion.maxTurnRateDegPerS`, `maxPitchDeg`, `turnGainPerS` 6, `turnSmoothingS` 0.1                                                  |
| Reaction lag                            | The summed desired velocity is low-pass filtered so discrete neighbour-set changes do not twitch the heading.                                                                                                                                                                                               | Fish visual response latencies are of order 0.1–0.2 s (startle responses are faster); value is **heuristic**.                        | `intentSmoothingS` 0.15                                                                                                                 |
| Banking                                 | Roll leans into the turn in proportion to tanh of the centripetal acceleration v·ω, smoothed.                                                                                                                                                                                                               | **Heuristic** visual cue; fish do roll into turns, but there is no clean scaling law used here.                                      | `maxBankRad` 35°, `bankReferenceAccel` 1 m/s², `bankSmoothingS` 0.2                                                                     |
| Wall avoidance (containment)            | A probe ahead (½ L + v·lookahead + turning radius) samples the SDF and is weighted by how fast the wall is closing along the heading, so a pane the fish runs parallel to fades out; a proximity term catches walls beside or behind. | Slide direction                         | The slide tangent is picked from the heading at first contact, then kept continuous (each step flips the new tangent to agree with the last) so a fish rounds a corner without reversing. | Speed during avoidance                  | While avoiding, the fish keeps at least a fraction of cruise speed (fish turn away from obstacles rather than stop), and when the summed intent nearly cancels it holds its heading instead of steering toward a near-zero vector.                                                                          | **Heuristic**; tested by the lone-tang stall test (< 1 % of steps below 0.3 × cruise).                                               | `avoidMinSpeedFraction` 0.5, `headingHoldFraction` 0.1                                                                                  |
| Hard collision constraint               | After integration, a fish closer than its radius to any surface is projected out along the SDF gradient (or reset), and speed into the wall is removed. Counted in `World.contacts`.                                                                                                                        | Standard position-based constraint; not a behaviour.                                                                                 | `collisionRadiusBodyLengths` 0.5                                                                                                        |

### Circling bound

Tangs boxed in by rock and glass sometimes turn through a full 360° within 4 s: a turn out of a
dead end, or a lap round a rock. The tests allow fewer than 2 such loops per 10 fish-minutes and
none that is a spin in place (path shorter than π body lengths). Measured over seeds 1–4: yellow
tang ≈ 1.5 and blue tang ≈ 0.7 loops per 10 fish-minutes.

## Not yet modelled

Feeding AI (perception cone, salience, competition, satiation and food preference) arrives with
the food particles in T13. Per-species tuning of the behaviour constants (flare timing, graze
interval, gulp rate) uses shared defaults until the species schema has fields for them.
